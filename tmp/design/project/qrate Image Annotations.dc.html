qrate — Image annotations

A region note is an ordinary note with a page and a box stored in image coordinates (0–1 on the upright image). It uses the note card, the note editor and History, so it follows zoom, pan, rotation and page turns without extra state. NEW marks new UI, TO CHECK marks copy or behaviour I guessed. Tweaks switch the panel themes and the viewer's pill theme; the stage stays black 85% either way.

  • Keys: bare keys only while the viewer holds focus, like R and W today. A annotate, N show or hide regions, [ and ] step through this page's regions, Delete removes the selected one (Select tool), Esc cancels a drawing or the composer. A, N, [ and ] are unused by the viewer now.
  • Region style: 1.5px dark outer and 1.5px light inner stroke, a 16px badge tucked into the top-left corner, 18px numbered dots for pins. Selection uses the theme primary on the badge and adds handles; nothing else gets the accent.
  • Overlap: larger regions draw underneath smaller ones and hover picks the smallest under the pointer. A badge that would cover another slides right along its top edge; badges that still collide merge into one pill ("5 6 7"). Hovering or selecting one region drops the others to 40%.
  • Missing file: the regions stay on the item. The viewer draws their outlines on a dashed frame of the last-known size, and cards swap the crop for a mini-map showing where the region sits. TO CHECK this assumes crops are cut from the file when drawn, not cached; if they are cached, cards can keep the real crop.
  • Hover card: one card for every note, on the image and in the grid (6). It shows the whole note, the author's name and when it was filed. There are no avatars. It replaces today's plain text tooltip on cells that carry a note.
  • Grid: a row with region notes uses the existing note marker.
