This tour names each part, so the rest of the tutorials can say "open the Details panel" and you
know where to look. Open the example project from the launcher and follow along.

## The grid

The grid is a spreadsheet. Each **row** is one record, a photo, a letter, a box, and each
**column** is one field, such as Title or Date. Click a cell and type to replace its value, or
press **Enter** to edit what is there. Copy and paste work between qrate and Excel or Google
Sheets.

Rows can sit inside other rows, the way items sit inside a series. A row with children has a
chevron in its row header: click it to fold the group away.

## Table and Gallery

Above the grid, the **Table** and **Gallery** tabs switch between rows of text and a wall of
thumbnails. **Ctrl+1** and **Ctrl+2** do the same. The gallery is the quickest way to spot a scan
that is upside down, blank, or attached to the wrong record.

## The panels

Panels open in three **docks**: left, right and bottom. Each has a button in the status bar that
shows or hides it.

| Panel | Starts in | What it is for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Details** | Left dock (right dock in Gallery) | The selected row's file and fields together. Edit a field here, or select several rows and edit them all at once. |
| **Problems** | Bottom dock | Everything qrate's checks found: spelling, dates, missing files, and more. Click a finding to jump to its cell. |
| **History** | Right dock | Every change to the project, who made it and when, with a way to put things back. |

The keyboard is quicker once you know it: **Ctrl+B** toggles the left dock, **Ctrl+Alt+B** the
right, and **``Ctrl+` ``** the bottom. **Ctrl+Shift+M** opens Problems wherever it is. **Shift+Esc**
zooms the panel you are in to fill the window, and pressing it again puts it back.

## The viewer

Click the preview in the Details panel to open the file full screen. You can zoom, rotate, page
through a PDF, and step to the previous or next row's file with the arrows. **Open in new window**
puts the file in a second window that follows your selection, which is handy on two screens.
**Esc** closes the viewer.

## The menus

The menu bar has **File**, **Edit**, **Insert**, **View**, **Data**, **Plugins** and **Help**. On
a Mac they live in the system menu bar. A few you will use often:

- **File ▸ Import Files or Folders…** adds more files as rows.
- **File ▸ Export** writes CSV, Excel and other formats.
- **Data ▸ Column Settings…** sets what each column holds.
- **Settings** (**Ctrl+,**) holds everything else.

## The status bar

The bar along the bottom is worth a glance now and then. Besides the panel buttons, it shows the
**Problems** count, and **New files (N)** when files have appeared in your files folder that no
row links to yet.
