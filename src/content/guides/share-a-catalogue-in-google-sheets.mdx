Small archives run on volunteers, and volunteers live in Google Sheets. qrate can publish your
catalogue to a sheet they can open on any computer or phone, and refresh it whenever you have made
progress.

The arrangement this guide sets up: **qrate is where the catalogue is edited, the sheet is where it
is read.** Sync goes one way, from qrate to the sheet, so the sheet never overwrites your work.

## Turn on Google Sheets

Google Sheets export is off until you turn it on, so nothing in qrate talks to Google by accident.

1. Open **Settings ▸ Google** and turn it on.
2. The first time you export, sign in to Google in your browser. qrate never sees your password,
   and it can only reach sheets it created or that you picked yourself.

No qrate account or server is involved: the rows go from your computer to your Google Drive.

## Publish to a new sheet

Choose **File ▸ Export ▸ New Google Sheet…**. qrate creates a sheet in your Drive and fills it.
Notes you left on cells become notes on the matching cells in the sheet, and notes on columns land on
the header cells.

Share the sheet from Google Sheets as you would any other, with **Viewer** access for most people.

## Keep it up to date

After a session of cataloguing, choose **File ▸ Export ▸ Sync to Google Sheet…**. qrate writes into
the sheet this project is linked to, or one you pick through Google's file picker.

Sync **replaces the whole first tab**: rows and columns beyond the project's are cleared, and so are
notes the last sync left. That is what keeps the sheet an exact copy. qrate writes the new values
before it clears anything, so a sync that fails partway never leaves the sheet empty.

## What volunteers can and cannot do

Because sync only goes one way, edits made in the sheet do **not** come back into qrate, and the
next sync overwrites them. Set expectations:

- **Give most people view access.** They can search, filter and read.
- **Collect corrections somewhere else**: a comment on the cell, a second tab, or a form. The first
  tab is replaced on every sync, but other tabs are not touched.
- **Make the change in qrate**, where the checks and the history are, then sync.

If volunteers do the describing in a sheet, import their work the other way instead: **File ▸ Import
Spreadsheet…** appends rows from a CSV or Excel export of the sheet, as one change you can undo.

## Start a project from a sheet

If the catalogue already lives in Google Sheets, choose **Google Sheet** on the launcher and paste
the link. qrate reads the sheet once to build a project, and dates come in as `YYYY-MM-DD`. From
then on qrate is the working copy, and you can sync back to the same sheet.
