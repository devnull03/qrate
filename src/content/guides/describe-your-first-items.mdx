This is the part of cataloguing that takes the hours: looking at an item and writing down what it
is. qrate cannot do the looking for you, but it can cut the clicking. By the end of this tutorial
you will have described a handful of items, filled a field across many rows at once, and left a
note for whoever checks your work.

## Type in the grid

Click a cell and start typing: what you type replaces the cell. Press **Enter** to keep it and move
on. To change part of a value instead, select the cell and press **Enter** first.

The rest works the way spreadsheets taught you. **Delete** clears a cell, copy and paste work
between qrate and Excel, and **Alt+Shift+Down** adds a row below the one you are on.

## Describe while you look

The grid is fast for short fields, but for a description you want to see the item. Select a row:
the **Details** panel shows its file at the top and its fields underneath. Click a field there,
type, and press **Enter**. Click the preview to open the file full screen and zoom in on a
signature or a postmark.

## Fill a field across many rows

Twenty letters from the same donor, all with the same creator? Don't type it twenty times.

1. Hold **Ctrl** and click each row, or click the first and **Shift**-click the last.
2. In the Details panel, a field whose values differ shows **Mixed** and how many values it holds.
3. Type the value once. It goes into every selected row.

One **Ctrl+Z** undoes it for all of them.

## Find and replace

**Ctrl+F** finds text in the grid, and **Ctrl+H** replaces it, one match or all of them. Use it for
the boring fixes, such as `Hfx` to `Halifax` across a whole column.

To work on part of the collection, filter it. Right-click a cell and choose **Filter by this
value** to see only the rows that share it. A filter only hides rows: export and the checks still
see everything.

## Leave a note

Not sure about a date? Don't guess silently. Right-click the cell and choose **Notes ▸ Add note…**,
or press **Shift+F2**, and write the question down. A corner marker shows on the cell, the note
appears on the **Notes** tab of the Problems panel, and it travels into Excel exports as a cell
comment. Set your name under **Settings ▸ Application ▸ Identity ▸ Author name** so people know
who asked.

## Undo, and the longer memory

**Ctrl+Z** undoes the last change, including rows and columns you added or moved. It covers the
current session.

For anything older, open the **History** panel from the status bar. It lists every change ever
made to the project, who made it and how, and lets you put one cell or the whole project back.
Right-click a cell and choose **Show edit history** to see just that cell's story.
