# qRate Mission Statement

## Vision

To empower archivists and cultural heritage professionals with tools that match the scale and importance of their work—preserving humanity's memory for future generations.

## Mission

qRate eliminates the technical bottlenecks in archival workflows by providing a unified, high-performance workspace that respects archival expertise while removing the limitations of traditional tools.

## The Problem We're Solving

Archivists are the guardians of cultural memory, yet they work with tools designed for accountants and office workers. When processing thousands of heritage items—photographs, documents, artifacts—they're forced to:

- Switch between 4-5 different applications
- Wait minutes for CSV files to open
- Risk data loss from crashes
- Manually track progress across multiple systems
- Fight against tools that freeze, corrupt data, or lose metadata

This isn't just inefficient—it's a barrier to preserving our collective history.

## Our Solution

qRate is built on three core principles:

### 1. Performance at Scale
- Open gigabyte files instantly
- Process millions of records without memory bloat
- Crash-proof transactions protect every edit
- Virtual scrolling provides constant performance

### 2. Standards-First Design
- Native support for RAD (Rules for Archival Description)
- MODS (Metadata Object Description Schema) ready
- Integration with controlled vocabularies (LCSH, TGM)
- Validation against archival best practices

### 3. Human-in-the-Loop AI
- AI suggests metadata, humans validate
- Flag inconsistencies (spelling, dates, formats)
- Learn from archivist corrections
- Augment expertise, don't replace it

## Core Values

**Sustainability**: Data stored in open formats (CSV/SQLite) ensures long-term access, outliving any single software platform.

**Local-First**: Your data stays on your machine. No cloud dependencies, no vendor lock-in, no privacy concerns.

**Throughput with Quality**: Batch operations enable speed without sacrificing the precision archival work demands.

**Respect for Expertise**: Archivists know their collections. qRate provides tools, not dictates.

**Accessibility**: Free, open-source, and designed to work on modest hardware.

## Why This Matters

Every day, archivists make thousands of small decisions that shape how future generations understand the past. A photograph's description, a document's date, a proper name's spelling—these aren't just data points. They're the threads that connect us to our history.

When the tools fail, the work suffers. When archivists spend more time fighting software than preserving heritage, we all lose.

qRate exists to flip that equation: minimal friction, maximum focus on the work that matters.

- AI assistance that learns from archival expertise
- Cultural institutions of all sizes able to digitize their collections

## Built For

- **Small archives** with limited technical resources

## The Technical Foundation

qRate isn't just faster—it's fundamentally different:

- Treats metadata as databases (SQLite), not text files
- Virtual scrolling eliminates memory constraints
- ACID transactions ensure crash-proof reliability
- Local-first architecture ensures data sovereignty
- Standards-ready from the ground up

## Long-Term Vision

Phase 1 (Current): High-performance metadata editing with SQLite backend
Phase 2: Media viewer integration for streamlined cataloging
Phase 3: AI-assisted metadata generation and validation
Phase 4: Full archival standards compliance (RAD, MODS, EAD)
Phase 5: Collaborative features for multi-user teams

But at every phase: Respect for archival expertise, commitment to open formats, and zero tolerance for data loss.
